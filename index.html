FirstFinder

A free, open-source catalog for people who collect things.

Visit firstfinder.app → View GitHub →

Situation

Collectors track receipts, purchase prices, and values in spreadsheets that get away from them, and the existing tools are built for dealers with thousands of items, not the hobbyist with forty books.

Product

I designed and built FirstFinder end to end. Take a photo and AI fills in the title, edition, printing, condition, and a search-grounded value range. It keeps receipts, cost basis, and sales in one ledger, works offline at book fairs, and lets collectors share a read-only collection page.
